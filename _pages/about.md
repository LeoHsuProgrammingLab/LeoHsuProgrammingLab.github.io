**Research Focus: 3D Computer Vision**, **Generative Modeling**, and **Computer Vision for Science**

I am a second-year master's degree student in Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University (TAMU). I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Cheng Zhang](https://czhang0528.github.io/) in TAMU Visual AI Lab, and we are now working on **taxanomy-aware fine-grained image generation**. Previously, we worked on **uncertainty-aware dynamic 3D reconstruction**. During 2025 summer, we also explored the video surveillance with real-time localization and multi-object tracking in constrained healthcare environment.

I was a graduate research assistant in the Communications and Multimedia Lab in Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Taiwan University (NTU), advised by Prof. [Cheng-Fu Chou](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~ccf/). We worked on intelligent health which improves hospital management by object detection & multi-object tracking.

I earned my bachelor's and master's degree in Department of Forestry and Resource Conservation at NTU, advised by Prof. [Tzeng Yih, Lam](https://forestry.ubc.ca/faculty-profile/tzeng-yih-lam/), and we worked on Android mobile camera application design for Horizontal Point Sampling in forestry.
