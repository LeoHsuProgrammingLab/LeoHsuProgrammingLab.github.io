### **Athlete Mindset**

> “The truth is you don’t need most stars, hype, or rankings. Just your discipline, heart, and people who believe in you. And you need to believe in your own ability.”
>
> — Fernando Mendoza, 2025 Heisman Trophy

Basketball has shaped me since high school. My years on the Changhua Senior High School team were [bittersweet](https://medium.com/@hooptaiwan/hbl%E7%94%B2%E7%B5%84%E5%85%89%E7%92%B0%E5%8F%8D%E6%80%9D-%E5%BD%B0%E5%8C%96%E9%AB%98%E4%B8%AD%E7%9A%84%E7%B4%A0%E4%BA%BA%E7%B1%83%E7%90%83%E5%A4%A2-ae4d086c9f85).

At NTU, I was a student-athlete and captained the varsity team, the [NTU Owls](https://www.facebook.com/ntubasketballteam/), from 2019 to 2021. We became the only team in Taiwan's history to reach Division 1 without a single basketball-specialized student ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cFGGtNWDhlA&list=PLUfT0s3t7xLRbK0RMdu0fdyOxNEkWIQGM&index=1), [article](https://www.sportsv.net/articles/82435)).

Off the court, I have been a Lakers fan since the 2007-08 season. Kobe Bryant and his Mamba Mentality are my touchstone, and after he retired I came to appreciate Rajon Rondo's game. I also love tactics design, especially zone defense, full-court press and press break, and 5-out motion offense.
