### **Athlete Mindset**

> “The truth is you don’t need most stars, hype, or rankings. Just your discipline, heart, and people who believe in you, and you need to believe in your own ability.”
>
> — Fernando Mendoza, 2025 Heisman Trophy

I was a student-athlete when I studied in bachelor's degree at NTU. I had been the captain of varsity team [NTU Owls](https://www.facebook.com/ntubasketballteam/) from 2019 to 2021. I led the team to be the only team in the Taiwan history without any basketball-specialized students in Division 1 ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cFGGtNWDhlA&list=PLUfT0s3t7xLRbK0RMdu0fdyOxNEkWIQGM&index=1), [article](https://www.sportsv.net/articles/82435)).

I have been a Lakers fan since the 2007-08 season. My favorite player is Kobe Bryant (Mamba Mentality). After he retired, I enjoyed Rajon Rondo's game. Besides, I love tactics design, especially for zone defense, full court press & break, and 5-out motion offense.
